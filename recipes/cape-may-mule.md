## Ingredients
- 2 oz Absolut Elyx vodka
- Fresh cranberries
- Ginger beer, to top
- Lime, for squeezing
- Ice

### Garnish
- Fresh cranberries

## Steps
1. Muddle fresh cranberries in a copper mule mug.
2. Fill the mug 2/3 of the way with ice.
3. Add the vodka, then top with ginger beer and fresh squeezed lime juice.
4. Garnish with cranberries.
