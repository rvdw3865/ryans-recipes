## Ingredients
- 4 oz Grey Goose vodka
- 1 oz organic maple syrup
- 1 sprig rosemary
- 5 oz pink grapefruit juice
- 1 pinch of salt

### Garnish
- 1 extra sprig rosemary

## Steps
1. In the bottom of a rocks glass, briefly crush the rosemary leaves into the maple syrup and salt.
2. Fill the glass with ice and add the vodka.
3. Top with fresh pink grapefruit juice and a little more salt, and stir well.
4. Garnish with an extra sprig of rosemary.
