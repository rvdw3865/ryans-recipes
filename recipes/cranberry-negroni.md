## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz Staple Gin
- 3/4 oz Campari
- 3/4 oz cranberry juice
- 3/4 oz red (sweet) vermouth
- 1 orange wedge

### Garnish
- Cranberries, 3 to 5 per drink
- 1 sprig fresh rosemary

## Steps
1. Combine the gin, Campari, cranberry juice and red vermouth in a rocks glass.
2. Squeeze in the juice from the orange wedge.
3. Add ice and stir to chill.
4. Garnish with the cranberries and a rosemary sprig.
