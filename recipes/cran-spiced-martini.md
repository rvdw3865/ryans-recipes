## Ingredients
- 2 oz Reyka Vodka
- 1 oz 100% cranberry juice
- 3/4 oz vanilla spiced syrup (below)

### Garnish
- Cranberries, on a skewer

### Vanilla spiced syrup
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
- 5 tsp allspice
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1/2 oz vanilla extract

## Steps

### Make the vanilla spiced syrup
1. Add the sugar, water, black pepper, allspice and cinnamon stick to a saucepan.
2. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 5 minutes.
3. Let cool completely.
4. Strain, then stir in the vanilla extract.

### Make the drink
1. Combine the vodka, cranberry juice and vanilla spiced syrup in a shaker with ice.
2. Shake, then strain into a martini glass.
3. Garnish with skewered cranberries.
