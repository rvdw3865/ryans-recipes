## Ingredients
- 2 oz cranberry juice
- 1 oz fresh lime juice
- 1 1/2 oz blanco tequila
- 1/2 oz Cointreau (optional)
- 1/4 oz simple syrup or agave syrup

### Garnish
- Salt, for the rim
- Fresh cranberries
- 1 lime wheel

## Steps
1. Rim a glass with salt and fill it with fresh ice cubes.
2. Combine the cranberry juice, lime juice, tequila, Cointreau and syrup in a shaker filled with ice.
3. Shake until the outside of the shaker is frosty and cold.
4. Pour into the salt-rimmed glass.
5. Garnish with cranberries and a lime wheel.
