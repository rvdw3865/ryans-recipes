## Ingredients
### Base
- 1 1/3 cups whole milk
- 1 1/3 cups heavy cream
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp light corn syrup
- 2 tbsp dry non-fat milk powder 
- 1/4 tsp xanthan gum
### Base Mix
- 4-6 pieces of ginger (I like using pre-peeled ginger pieces that I keep in the freezer. Special thanks to Fallow for this trick.)
- Ghirardelli 60% Cacao Dark Chocolate Bar
- 1/4 cup Ghirardelli 100% Cocoa Dutch Process Cocoa Powder
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
### Mix-ins
- 6 Border Chocolate Ginger Cookies (refrigerated)

## Steps

### Prepare the base
1. Put the whole milk, sugar, corn syrup, milk powder, and xanthan gum into a pot and heat over medium low heat.
    - 1 1/3 cup of milk
    - 1/2 cup of sugar
    - 2 tbsp corn syrup
    - 2 tbsp non-fat dry milk powder
    - 1/4 tsp of xanthan gum
2. After 5 mins, whisk until it's mostly dissolved, then use an immersion blender to dissolve all of the ingredients into the base.
3. Resume heating on the stove until it's steaming.

### Make the base mix
1. Break up your chocolate bar and set aside for later.
    - 1 Ghirardelli 60% cacao dark chocolate bar
2. In a separate pan or pot, put the granulated sugar and water over medium heat. 
    - 1/4 cup granulated sugar
    - 1/4 cup water
3. Wait until your sugar has dissolved into your water and then add in your cocoa powder. It should begin thickening and dissolving into the simple syrup.
    - 1/4 cup Ghirardelli Dutch Process Cocoa Powder
4. Add your chocolate bar and mix until dissolved. The mixture should be very thick but still scoop-able and spreadable. If it's too thick just gradually add a little more water.
5. Using a rubber spatula, transfer your chocolate mix into the base.

### Finish the base
1. Using an immersion blender, completely blend in your chocolate mix into the base.
2. Get your ginger, using a microplane, grate your ginger pieces into the base.
    - 4-6 pieces of peeled ginger
3. Using an immersion blender, completely blend the ginger into the base.
4. Taste and adjust adding more ginger as necessary.
5. Place the base into the fridge and let chill overnight.

### Churn it up!
1. Place your ice cream bowl into your ice cream machine and let chill for 5-10 mins.
2. While your bowl chills, take your base and pass it through a fine mesh sieve, removing any leftover pieces of ginger that may not have blended successfully.
3. Once the bowl is sufficiently cooled, pour your strained base into the ice cream machine and begin churning on Ice Cream mode.
4. About 10 mins into the churning process, cut up your border bars into bite sized pieces to mix in.
    - 6 Refrigerated Border Bar Cookies
5. Once your ice cream machine signifies it is ready for mix-ins to be added, add in your border bar pieces.
6. Finish churning and place your ice cream directly into the freezer.
