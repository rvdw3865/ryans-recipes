## Ingredients
- 2 oz Hendrick's Gin
- 1 1/2 oz apple cider
- 1/2 oz lemon juice
- 1 1/2 oz ginger beer

### Garnish
- Apple slices
- Fresh mint

## Steps
1. Fill a rocks glass with ice.
2. Add the gin, apple cider, lemon juice and ginger beer, and stir well.
3. Garnish with apple slices and mint.
