## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz Belvedere vodka
- 1/2 oz green Chartreuse
- 1 oz lemon juice
- 1 oz simple syrup

### Garnish
- Cranberries
- 1 sprig fresh rosemary

## Steps
1. Add the vodka, Chartreuse, lemon juice and simple syrup to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake, then strain into a Collins glass.
3. Garnish with cranberries on the bottom of the glass and a rosemary sprig on top.
