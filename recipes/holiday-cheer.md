## Ingredients
- 2 oz D'USSÉ VSOP Cognac
- 3 oz cloudy apple cider
- 1 oz pomegranate juice
- 1/2 oz cinnamon simple syrup

## Steps
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake until cold.
3. Strain into a rocks glass filled with ice.
