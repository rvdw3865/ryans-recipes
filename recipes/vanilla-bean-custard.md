## Ingredients

- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 cup whole milk
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 6 large egg yolks

## Steps

1. Warm the cream, milk, half the sugar, the salt and the vanilla seeds and pod in a saucepan over medium heat until steaming. Don't let it boil.
2. Whisk the yolks with the remaining sugar until pale.
3. Slowly pour about a cup of the hot cream into the yolks while whisking, then pour everything back into the pan.
4. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the custard coats the back of a spoon (170–175°F).
5. Strain into a clean bowl, cool over an ice bath, then cover and chill at least 4 hours or overnight.
6. Churn until it looks like soft-serve, then pack into a container and freeze until firm.

## Notes

Freeze the machine's bowl for at least 24 hours first. You can use 1 tbsp vanilla extract instead of the bean; stir it in after straining.
